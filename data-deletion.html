Data deletion

Last updated: November 2026

Data on your phone and Google Drive

Your data is stored locally on your phone and, if backup is enabled, in an “EquiRiding” folder on your personal Google Drive. To delete it:

  1. In the app, sign out of Google from the Backup screen.
  2. Delete the “EquiRiding” folder from your Google Drive.
  3. Uninstall the app: all local data is erased.

Horse-sharing account

If you used horse sharing, a sharing account exists on our servers (Supabase, European Union). It holds your first name or nickname, your Google email if you linked it, and the shared data (shared horse profiles, calendar, notes).

  1. In the app: Settings → Horse sharing → Delete my sharing account. The account and all related data are deleted immediately and permanently.
  2. Or email equiriding.app@gmail.com, from the linked address or stating your sharing name: deletion is done within 30 days.

No data is kept after deletion.

Contact: equiriding.app@gmail.com
