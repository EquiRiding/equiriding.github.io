Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Publisher

Sébastien Richet – EquiRiding app. Contact: equiriding.app@gmail.com

Data stored on your device

The data you enter (horses, sessions, care, vaccinations, feeding, rides, photos) is stored locally on your phone. It only leaves the device in the cases described below.

Horse sharing

This feature is optional. If you use it, the data needed for sharing is hosted by Supabase (servers in the European Union, Ireland):

  • an account identifier (anonymous by default) and the first name or nickname you enter;
  • for the owner: the shared horse's profile (without photo), its care, vaccinations, feeding and planned sessions;
  • slots, requests, notes and unavailability periods of the shared calendar;
  • for the sharer: the sessions they plan on the shared horse;
  • the owner's subscription status (active or not), used to grant access to their sharers.

This data is only visible to the horse's owner and the sharers they linked themselves. It is deleted when sharing ends or on request.

Google account (optional)

You can link your sharing account to Google to recover it on a new phone. EquiRiding then only receives your Google email address, used to identify you. No other access to your Google account is requested.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If enabled, a copy of your data is sent to your own Google Drive, in an app-specific folder. EquiRiding has no access to these files. Horses shared with you as a sharer are not backed up.

Location

GPS is only used to record rides and for local weather. Positions stay on your device and are not shared with third parties.

Third-party services

  • Weather: Open-Meteo (no personal data, only approximate coordinates).
  • Maps: OpenStreetMap.
  • Subscriptions: Google Play and the App Store, under their own terms. EquiRiding has no access to your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are generated on your device. Sharing alerts (horse unavailable, new return date) are computed by the app from the shared data.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, delete and port your data. To exercise it, write to equiriding.app@gmail.com.
